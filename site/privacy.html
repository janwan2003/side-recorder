Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-20.

Tapeline collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension stores

Your current test project (test names, steps, locators, values you typed while recording) is kept in the browser's local extension storage so it is still there when you reopen the editor. It never leaves your computer unless you save it to a file or export code yourself. Removing the extension deletes this storage.

Why it asks for access to websites

To record a test, Tapeline must see your clicks and typing in the page you are testing, and to replay a test it must click and type in that page. It asks for this access when you first press Record or Run. It reads pages only while you record, replay, pick an element or find a locator, and only in the tab it opened or you started it from.

Permissions

  • Access to websites (requested when you first record or run): to record and replay in the page under test.
  • scripting: to add the recorder and player to that page.
  • tabs: to open the page under test and follow it as it navigates.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: to keep your project locally between sessions.
  • contextMenus: for the right-click "Assert text" and similar items while recording.

The converter page

The .side to code converter runs entirely in your browser. The file you choose is not uploaded.
