How the conversion works

    
Each step becomes the equivalent call: open becomes a page visit, click a click, type a fill, assertions become the framework's checks, and if/while/times/forEach become real loops and conditions. Locators like id=, name=, css=, xpath= and linkText= map to the framework's own. Steps with no equivalent stay in the code as TODO comments, so nothing is lost silently. To record new tests and replay them in the browser, get the Tapeline extension.
